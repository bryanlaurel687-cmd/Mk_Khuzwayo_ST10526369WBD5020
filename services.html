Our Services

Programmes designed to make reading accessible, exciting and meaningful.

Storytelling Sessions

Interactive storytelling experiences that encourage children to listen, imagine, participate and discover the joy of stories.

Reading Programmes

Reading opportunities that help children build confidence and develop a lifelong love of books.

Literacy Workshops

Literacy workshops help children develop reading skills through guided activities and learning experiences.

Book Donations

Free books help children and communities build their own access to reading materials.

School Partnerships

Schools can request visits from the mobile library to support learners where library facilities are limited.

Volunteer Training

Volunteers support reading programmes and bus operations while helping create positive literacy experiences.

Who We Serve

The primary audience is children between the ages of 4 and 14 from underprivileged communities.

We also reach parents and caregivers who want to encourage reading at home.

Local schools that lack adequate library facilities are an important audience.

Community centres and youth groups are also served by the organisation.

Volunteers aged 18 and above who are interested in literacy development are another important audience.

Corporate sponsors and individual donors support educational initiatives and help the organisation grow.
