About Wheels of Wonder

Wheels of Wonder is a mobile library that brings books, storytelling and literacy programmes directly to children and communities across Gauteng.

Our mobile library makes reading more accessible by travelling to different communities and providing children with opportunities to discover new stories, learn new skills and develop a love for reading.

Our Story

Wheels of Wonder was created to help make books and literacy activities available to children who may not have easy access to a traditional library.

Our mobile library visits schools, community centres, parks and other community locations. Through these visits, children can explore books, participate in storytelling sessions and take part in literacy activities.

What We Believe

Our Mission

To provide children with access to books, reading activities and literacy programmes that support their education.

Our Vision

To create communities where every child has the opportunity to read, learn and develop their knowledge and skills.

Our Community

We work together with children, parents, schools, volunteers and community members to promote reading and education.

Our Values

Our work is guided by values that help us create a positive learning environment for children.

  • Education and Learning
  • Love of Reading
  • Community Support
  • Teamwork
  • Respect
  • Helping Others

Join the Wheels of Wonder Community
