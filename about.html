About Wheels of Wonder

Bringing books, stories and opportunities to children across Gauteng.

Our History

Wheels of Wonder Mobile Library was established in February 2018 by Thandi Mokoena, a former primary school educator from Soshanguve, Pretoria.

After twelve years in the classroom, Thandi observed that many of her learners struggled with reading because they lacked access to books at home. The nearest public library was 15 kilometres away, and many families could not afford to purchase books.

In 2017, Thandi sold her personal vehicle for R45,000 and purchased a second-hand delivery van. She filled it with 200 donated books and began visiting communities where children had limited access to reading materials.

By 2019, Wheels of Wonder had officially registered as a non-profit organisation and secured its first grant.

In 2022, a local business donated a converted double-decker bus, which was painted by community artists and transformed into a fully functional mobile library with bookshelves, comfortable seating and a dedicated reading area.

Today, Wheels of Wonder reaches over 8,000 children annually across 15 communities in Gauteng. The organisation employs 8 staff members, works with 56 active volunteers and maintains a collection of over 4,500 books.

Our Mission

To bring the joy of reading to every child, regardless of their circumstances, by creating magical storytelling experiences that inspire a lifelong love of learning.

Our Vision

A South Africa where every child has access to books, stories, and the opportunity to dream.
